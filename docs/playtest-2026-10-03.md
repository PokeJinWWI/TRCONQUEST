## Goal 1: Eris

1. **W, bug/design.** Founding a colony in your own home system counts as building "inside the borders" of every nation that shares it.
   - Did: Colony Ship > Colonize… > Eris; colony founded day 287.
   - Expected: no diplomatic cost for settling a free world of the system your capital is in.
   - Actual: two notifications, "Imperial State of Mars built a colony inside Earth's borders at Sol. Earth's opinion of it falls by 20." and the same for Venus. Earth declared war on Mars 5 days later (day 292). Every Sol nation does this to the others: 10 encroachment records by day 367, more after (Venus on Europa, Ganymede, Haumea, Io...).
   - This is the encroachment rule added this session meeting a shared home system (Sol is contested between Earth, Mars and Venus from day 1).
2. **W, bug.** The Colonize chooser offers gas giants and Mercury: Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune are listed as worlds to settle, with enabled buttons. Not tried (I did not click one), so I don't know if founding succeeds.
3. **P, contradicts CLAUDE.md and the brief.** Eris needed no Starbase. `canColonize` now exempts the nation's home system ("A nation's own home system needs no Starbase to colonize in"); CLAUDE.md's Colonies section still says a colony needs "a system where its nation has a finished Starbase".
4. **P, confusing UI.** The ship panel's "Jump Risk" row always reads "50% new · 10% charted", whatever the real risk is (I saw real values from 0.4% to 100%).
5. **P, confusing UI.** After picking a world by hand the panel shows a button "Cancel · choose myself", which reads as if automation had picked it.
6. **P, observation.** Venus declared war on Mars on day 246, before I had done anything to it; Luna was then invaded ("yours 55 · enemy 934"). I kept Luna, but an early two-front war against the player with no warning is harsh for an early-game loop.
7. **P, tedious.** Any fight I am part of drops the clock to OPS (1 day/s) or TAC (1 s/s). A hostile ship attacking my unarmed Science Ship at Barnard's Star switched the game to TAC; I had to click STRAT to keep going. Working as documented, but with wars running it makes waiting slow.
8. **P, layout.** Notifications stack over the top of the Outliner (its title and search box are covered while toasts are up).
9. **P, layout.** At a narrow window (519 px wide) the top bar overlaps: the game title sits on top of the treasury and resource readouts.

## Goal 2: Barnard's Star

10. **W, missing warning.** "Survey system" on another star starts a risky jump with no confirmation.
    - Did: Science Ship selected, right-click Barnard's Star > Survey system.
    - Expected: the "Risky jump" confirmation (the jump was 18.5%, the line is 5%).
    - Actual: the job started at once; the ship jumped when unpaused. It survived by luck.
11. **W, missing reason text.** With the Colony Ship at Barnard's Star and my Starbase there still under construction, the chooser listed only Sol worlds and said "Nothing here to settle." It never says the Barnard worlds are waiting on the Starbase.
12. **P.** The chooser is one long list (33 buttons) with the ship's own system's worlds after 20+ Sol worlds, and no system names.
13. **P, missing info.** Neither the "Build Starbase" button nor the star menu item says what it costs in influence (it took 30 here, 56 in the other cluster).
14. **P, wrong text.** The "Move to Barnard's Star" menu tip read "Jump risk: 5% chance the ship is lost (it grows with distance and the destination's mass)" on a charted lane. The real value is 4.6% (rounded up to exactly the warning line), and on a charted lane the mass no longer counts.
15. **P, missing notification.** Nothing announces a Starbase starting or finishing; I had to read its ready day from state.
16. **Cheat finding (W).** The legitimate route to the first Starbase is about two years: Orbital Construction needs 110 Physics points at +5.2/mo, then 60 Engineering at +2.6/mo ("about 23 months" in the queue). With the clock pinned to 1 day/s by the wars this was too slow to play, so I used Free Research.
17. **P, Free Research cheat.** With it on, the research queue still shows the old cost and ETA ("70 pts, about 11 months"), queued techs do not complete on their own (each needs a click on "0 pts"), and the queue still held the two techs after I researched them. I did not check whether the queue clears at the next month.
18. **P, Debug Console.** It has no cheat for resources, influence, time or ending a war, and its System list only has the Solar Neighbourhood's stars (nothing can be spawned in another cluster).

## Goal 3: Apus Marches (nearest cluster, 2.5 kly)

19. **B without a cheat.** At Hyperdrive Mk I the cluster panel read "Jump: 100% chance the ship is lost". The legitimate fix is Hyperdrive Mk II to V (150, 375 and more Engineering points, plus hyperium), far beyond what I could reach in play. I used Free Research; at Mk V the jump read 2.2%, and 0.4% once the lane was charted. The panel gives no hint of what would make the jump safer.
20. **W, bug.** The cluster panel's jump risk goes stale. After researching Mk II to V it still read 100% with the ship selected; it only changed to 2.2% after I selected another cluster and came back.
21. **W, confusing UI.** Picking a cluster in the Outliner clears the ship selection, so the "Jump" risk row disappears; the ship has to be selected again afterwards.
22. **W, confusing UI (not verified with a real mouse).** A right-click on the selected cluster's own marker and label did nothing; the order only registered as a right-click on the map itself at that spot. My clicks were synthetic events, so this needs a check by hand.
23. **W, bug.** "Go To" in the ship panel, pressed in the galaxy view for a ship inside Apus Marches, did not open that cluster's map; the view stayed on the galaxy. Workaround: cluster panel > Enter Neighborhood.
24. **P, confusing status.** A ship at a cluster's entry point reads "In Deep Space" (no cluster name), and a Science Ship with a survey job waiting on its hyperdrive cooldown shows the same, with no "waiting for the drive" text.
25. **P.** The nearest cluster's marker overlaps the Solar Neighbourhood's at the default galaxy zoom, so the lane between them cannot be seen and the cluster is hard to click.
26. **P.** Opening the cluster's map lands far zoomed out: its seven stars sit in a clump with overlapping labels.
27. **W, bug.** Outliner > Colonies: rows for colonies with long details lose their name. Two rows showed only "micro-colony · Barnard's" and "micro-colony · Apus March" with no body name visible.
28. **P, missing info.** The "Move to Apus Marches Delta" menu item had an empty tip (no jump-risk line) from the entry point. I did not find out why.
29. **P.** The cluster panel shows "Distance from core" but not the distance from the selected ship or from home.

## How I played, and what that means for these findings

- A temporary read-only probe exposed the stores so I could read state; it is reverted.
- I paused and unpaused through the store rather than the Space key, and watched the clock from the store.
- The browser pane was narrow (519 px), so I emulated a 1280x800 viewport. Real mouse clicks did not land reliably under emulation, so almost every click was sent as a DOM event to the game's own buttons, map markers and canvas. The game's handlers ran, but this is not the same as a hand on the mouse.
